I'm a final-year **Computer Science & Engineering** student at **Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST)**, Dhaka.

I love building for the web, from clean, responsive interfaces to the logic that powers them behind the scenes. I care about readable code, thoughtful design, and shipping things people can actually open and use.
